# Known open defects

Defects present in the current tree and not yet fixed, most severe first. Each entry names the cause, the affected code, what it costs today, and what would raise its severity.

## The alpha convention is held by the buffer, but composites and colour fills still take the caller's word for it
Every `PixelBuffer` holds its pixels in one `AlphaMode`, fixed by the factory that makes it - `create`, `ofStraight` and `wrap` answer straight colour (each colour at full strength, what a file holds), `canvas` and `ofPremultiplied` premultiplied colour (each colour already scaled by its alpha, what drawing source-over onto a transparent surface leaves) - and an image reports the convention its frames' buffers hold. The operations that read one buffer read its convention: `resize`, `applyFxaa`, `multiplyAlpha`, `invert`, `toAlphaMode`, `toBufferedImage` and the writers. The operations that lay one buffer or one colour onto another do not yet, so a caller still has to name the right formula by hand:
- **Composites** - `blit`, `blitScaled`, `blitScaledFxaa`, `blitTinted`, and `PixelGraphics`' `drawImage` forms, which lay with `BlendMode.NORMAL` - apply the caller's `BlendMode` to the stored ints whatever either buffer holds. A straight see-through image laid with `BlendMode.PREMULTIPLIED` onto a canvas still stores its straight ints: `505000FF` stays `505000FF` and encodes as `50FF00FF`, where `NORMAL`, the formula for a straight source over a premultiplied destination, stores `50190050`. `lerp` mixes two buffers' ints as held whichever conventions they hold, and `PixelDiff` reads both of its inputs as straight colour.
- **Colour fills** - `fill` and both `fillRect` forms write or blend the colour as given, so a straight colour filled into a canvas is read as premultiplied: `F0100010` written to a canvas stands for `F0110011`, not the tooltip fill whose premultiplied form is `F00F000F`. `Background` and `FrameNormalizer` work around it by filling a straight buffer and converting it.
- **`wrap(BufferedImage)`** copies a `TYPE_INT_ARGB_PRE` or `TYPE_4BYTE_ABGR_PRE` image and un-premultiplies it to straight colour, where it could share the array as a premultiplied buffer, so `wrap(canvas.toBufferedImage())` is a converted copy rather than the canvas.

The direction: a composite lays the source in the destination's convention, taking the source-over formula from the two buffers, so the misread above cannot be written; a colour argument is straight colour, laid in the convention of the buffer it is filled into; and `wrap` reads the convention off the image, `TiffImageReader` and `ImageFactory.fromImages` converting what they read to one convention. HIGH once a consumer lays a straight see-through image onto a canvas with a convention-named mode.

- Affected: `src/main/java/dev/simplified/image/PixelBuffer.java` (`blit`, `blitScaled`, `blitScaledFxaa`, `blitTinted`, `lerp`, `fill`, `fillRect`, `wrap`), `src/main/java/dev/simplified/image/BufferedImageUnpacker.java` (`intArgbPre`, `fourByteAbgrPre`, `viaDrawImage`), `src/main/java/dev/simplified/image/pixel/PixelDiff.java` (`of`), `src/main/java/dev/simplified/image/pixel/PixelGraphics.java` (`drawImage`), `src/main/java/dev/simplified/image/pixel/Background.java` (`composite`), `src/main/java/dev/simplified/image/frame/FrameNormalizer.java` (`normalize`), `src/main/java/dev/simplified/image/codec/tiff/TiffImageReader.java`, `src/main/java/dev/simplified/image/ImageFactory.java` (`fromImages`)
- Severity: **MEDIUM**
- Type: **RISK**
- Status: **OPEN**

## PixelGraphics' fills and shapes overwrite the pixel while it reports `SrcOver`
`PixelGraphics` extends `Graphics2D`, starts with `AlphaComposite.SrcOver` and hands back from `getComposite` whatever `setComposite` stored, but no colour draw reads it. `fillDeviceRect` sends every rectangle and every row a fill, outline or shape lays at once to `PixelBuffer#fillRect(int, int, int, int, int)`, an overwrite, and `setPixelSafe` sends each pixel a line, oval, arc (`fillArc` included) or rounded corner plots alone to `setPixel`; only the `drawImage` forms composite, with `BlendMode.NORMAL`. A translucent colour therefore replaces what lies beneath it rather than laying over it: a `0x80FF0000` fill over a premultiplied `F00F000F` stores `80FF0000`, where `BlendMode.NORMAL`, and Java2D's `SrcOver` on a `TYPE_INT_ARGB_PRE` image, store `F8870007`. Several draws also plot a pixel more than once in one call, which an overwrite hides and a composite would not, since a pixel laid twice takes the colour twice: `drawRect`'s four edges share their corners (and lie one on another when it has no width or height), `drawOval` plots the points on each axis twice, `drawRoundRect`'s corner arcs repeat the end pixels of its straight edges (with both arc sizes below 2 its edges meet at their corners as `drawRect`'s do), `fillRoundRect`'s body shares its top and bottom rows with the corner bands, `drawPolyline` and `drawPolygon` repeat each shared vertex, `fillPolygon` can repeat a node two spans share, `drawArc`'s sampled points can repeat, and `draw(Shape)` repeats the point where two of its segments meet, a curve being drawn as many short lines. No workspace caller draws a translucent fill or shape through a `PixelGraphics`: asset-renderer's text decorations go through minecraft-text's own `fillEffect`, and minecraft-text calls none of the fills it inherits. HIGH once a caller draws a translucent fill or shape through one.

- Affected: `src/main/java/dev/simplified/image/pixel/PixelGraphics.java:124-127` (`setPixelSafe`), `:150-162` (`fillDeviceRect`), `:291-298` (`fillRect`, `clearRect`), `:301-692` (outlines and shapes), `:699-727` (`drawQuadCurve`, `drawCubicCurve`), `:966-973` (`setComposite`, `getComposite`)
- Severity: **MEDIUM**
- Type: **BUG**
- Status: **OPEN**

## No ICC / Exif / XMP metadata model; WebP ICC profiles are dropped
`WebPChunk.Type` names `ICCP`, `EXIF` and `XMP `, but no reader or writer carries them and `ImageData` has no metadata field, so colour profiles and Exif are lost on every WebP round trip. MEDIUM once a wide-gamut or HDR format depends on its colour metadata.

- Affected: `src/main/java/dev/simplified/image/codec/webp/WebPChunk.java:39-54`, `src/main/java/dev/simplified/image/codec/webp/WebPImageReader.java`, `src/main/java/dev/simplified/image/codec/webp/WebPImageWriter.java`, `src/main/java/dev/simplified/image/ImageData.java`
- Severity: **LOW**
- Type: **GAP**
- Status: **OPEN**
